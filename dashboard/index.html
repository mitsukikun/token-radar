What you are looking at. Every protocol in the snapshot that records no token yet is ranked by an implied value: its TVL priced at the median market-cap-per-TVL of the live-token protocols in its own category, adjusted for seven-day TVL growth, for how long it has run with no token, and for how many chains it lives on. The watchlist is the floor of this board, never the ceiling — the board also ranks the early finds the watchlist does not name.

What it is not. An implied value is a model, not a price and not a promise: a protocol with no token today may never launch one. “No token recorded” means the snapshot carries no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol for it — it does not mean the protocol has no token. Wrapped, bridged and staked-asset products are not ranked at all: their TVL is the asset itself and the receipt token already exists, so there is nothing left to launch — the Set aside view lists them. Read the risk score next to every row before you farm anything.
